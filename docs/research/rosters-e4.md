# Elite Four and Champion rosters - rebalanced

Six-Pokemon teams for the Sinnoh Elite Four and Champion Cynthia, rebuilt for the raised
cap table (`data/level_caps.json`), Mega Evolution as the only gimmick, and a Gen 4
(national dex 387-493) ace on every boss.

Generated by the rebalance workflow. Scratch work: `tools/_work/rebalance/`.

**Conventions used in every table below**

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lv** | The ace sits exactly AT the checkpoint cap; the rest sit within 3 levels below it. |
| **Ability** | On a Mega row this is the **Mega form's** ability, not the base species' - the base ability is irrelevant the moment it Megas. |
| **Item** | A Mega'd boss Pokemon holds its stone and therefore **loses its normal held item** (mega-design.md rule B7) - a real, automatic difficulty offset. |
| **Order** | Rows are in send-out order. The Mega'd member is always sent **immediately before the ace**, or is the ace. It Megas on the turn it is sent out (rule B6), never as a low-HP surprise. |

Every species, ability and move below was validated programmatically against
`data/species.json`, `data/learnsets.json`, `data/abilities.json` and `data/moves.json`
(`tools/_work/rebalance/check.py`). The only abilities that do **not** appear in
`data/abilities.json` under their base species are the five Mega abilities, which is expected.

## Balance review

### 1. Aaron is the fairness outlier: **+6 over the cap in force**

The cap table raises the cap to 96 on `flag:aaron_defeated`. The cap actually **in force when the
player walks into Aaron** is therefore still **90** (from `badge:beacon`). Aaron's 93-96 team is up
to six levels above anything the player is allowed to have, while every other League member is
only **+1**:

| Fight | Cap in force | Boss levels | Player deficit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aaron** | **90** | 93-96 | **-6** |
| Bertha | 96 | 94-97 | -1 |
| Flint | 97 | 95-98 | -1 |
| Lucian | 98 | 96-99 | -1 |
| Cynthia | 99 | 100 | -1, all six |

**As written, Aaron is the hardest fight in the game relative to the player, not Cynthia** - the
opposite of the intended shape, and it lands on the boss with the weakest ace. Two clean fixes,
owner's call:

- **Preferred:** move the 96 raise onto `badge:beacon`, so Volkner's checkpoint reads cap 96 and
  the player enters the League level-matched. Every roster in this document stays exactly as written.
- **Alternative:** drop Aaron's band to 87-90 with his ace at 90. This costs the clean
  96/97/98/99/100 final ladder and makes the first League fight feel like a step down from Volkner.

### 2. Requirement 5 vs. the cap table - the player cannot be level 100 *for* Cynthia

Requirement 5 is that natural progression brings the player to 100 by the Cynthia fight. Under the
authoritative table the 100 cap is raised by `flag:hall_of_fame`, i.e. **after she is beaten**, so
the player fights her at a maximum of 99 while all six of her team are 100. That +1 across the
board is a good reading of "hardest fight by a clear margin" and this roster is built for it. If
the owner wants requirement 5 literally, Lucian's defeat should grant 100 rather than 99.
**Flagged, not changed** - the cap table was handed over as authoritative.

### 3. Bertha's Mega Excadrill is blocked on an unwritten engine hook

`piercing-drill` is a **Pokemon Champions**-exclusive ability (Legends Z-A has no ability system).
Its effect text *is* published - it pierces Protect for 1/4 damage - so this is ordinary engine
work rather than a guess, but mega-design.md rule **B9** forbids shipping a boss whose Mega runs an
inert ability, because that is a difficulty regression the player cannot see. Fallbacks in order:

1. **Mega Steelix** (`steelixite`, Steel/Ground, `sand-force`, tier 1) - the mega doc's own named
   fallback, identical substitution shape. Costs flavour by re-using Byron's Mega, but Bertha
   already runs permanent sand, so Sand Force is live from turn one - arguably a better fight.
2. **Mega Swampert** (Water/Ground, `swift-swim`) - tier-1 ability, but Swift Swim is dead weight
   without rain and this fight is built on sand. Not recommended.

### 4. Deliberately strong, with the dial to turn if it plays badly

| Thing | Why it is in | Dial |
|---|---|---|
| **Gliscor** Poison Heal + Toxic Orb, L95 | The Ground specialist's unkillable pivot | Protect and Substitute were **deliberately left off** so it cannot stall out the clock. If it still drags, swap `toxic-orb` for `yache-berry`. |
| **Togekiss** Serene Grace + Air Slash, L100 | Canon Cynthia; the 60% flinch is her signature | The least fun mechanic in the gauntlet. If flinch-chaining reads as cheap rather than hard, swap `air-slash` for `dazzling-gleam`. |
| **Hippowdon** Sand Stream + Smooth Rock | Whole-fight weather that chips the player's team and is never re-set | Drop the Smooth Rock for `leftovers`: 5 turns instead of 8. |
| **Flint's** Heat Rock sun | Makes Solar Power and two Solar Beams real instead of decorative | Remove `heat-rock`; sun then lasts 5 turns and the player can stall it out. |
| **Roserade** Focus Sash + Toxic Spikes, L100 | Guarantees the hazards go down once | Remove the Sash. Note that `sleep-powder` is **not** in Roserade's learnset in `data/learnsets.json`, so `stun-spore` is used - which is also the fairer move. |
| **Ting-Lu** Vessel of Ruin | A -25% Special Attack aura for Bertha's entire fight | Swap for Clodsire (Poison/Ground, Unaware) or Great Tusk (Ground/Fighting) if the aura is too swingy. |
| **Alakazam** Magic Guard + Life Orb, L97 | No recoil, no hazard damage, no status damage | Canon-legal and iconic; if it over-performs, Focus Sash instead. |

### 5. Fair-play check - the player can own all of this first

Every stone the Elite Four uses is reachable before the League: `scizorite` T5, `excadrite` T4,
`houndoominite` T2, `galladite` T5. Only Cynthia's `garchompite` is withheld, and she hands it over
immediately after the credits. **The Elite Four shows the player nothing they could not already
own** - the Champion shows them exactly one thing, then gives it to them.

### 6. Minor notes

- Aaron's ace **Drapion is 500 BST** - the weakest ace in the League, on the fight that currently
  has the largest level advantage. Give him the top AI tier and a Full Restore so the fight earns
  its position instead of leaning on the +6.
- Cynthia carries **two `leftovers`** (Togekiss, Milotic). Fine for a trainer party; if the
  pipeline ever enforces unique held items per party, move Togekiss to `sitrus-berry`.
- **Levels are permuted, species are barely touched.** Vanilla members kept: Aaron 5/5,
  Bertha 4/5 (Golem substituted per rule B5), Flint 5/5, Lucian 4/5 (Mr. Mime upgraded to Mr.
  Rime), Cynthia 6/6. The sixth slots added are Volcarona (Gen 5), Ting-Lu (Gen 9), Ceruledge
  (Gen 9) and Hatterene (Gen 8) - the cross-generation selling point, one per Elite Four member,
  and none on the Champion, whose canon team is already six.

### 7. Mapping to `data/rom/trainers.json` (contract section 7)

This document uses `species` (name) plus `dexId` for readability. The pipeline emits contract
shape, where `species` is the **dex id** and the entry carries `level`, `moves`, `item`, `ability`
and `nature`. Natures are `null` for all thirty Pokemon. `isMega` is not a contract field - it is
expressed by the Pokemon holding the Mega Stone in `item`, which the engine resolves through
`can_mega_evolve()`; the boss AI Megas on the turn the holder is sent out (rule B6).
