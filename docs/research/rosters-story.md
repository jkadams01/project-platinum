# Story-boss rosters — Barry, Team Galactic, Cyrus

Generated by `tools/_work/rebalance/build_story_rosters.py`. Companion to the raised
cap table in `data/level_caps.json` (start 18 → Cynthia 100). Six Pokemon per boss,
no exceptions. Every ace is a **Sinnoh-native (Gen 4, dex 387–493)** species sitting
**exactly at the cap in force**; the rest sit within 3 levels below it.

## The rule set applied here

| Rule | How it lands on these bosses |
|---|---|
| Exactly 6 Pokemon | All 18 rosters below. Vanilla parties were 1–5. |
| Ace = Gen 4, at the cap | Monferno/Infernape (Barry), Purugly (Mars), Skuntank (Jupiter), Toxicroak (Saturn), Porygon-Z (Charon), Honchkrow → Weavile → Giratina (Cyrus). |
| Mega only after the Ring | The Ring is Dawn/Lucas's gift **after Gym 3 (Fantina)**. Nothing before that Megas: Barry R1/R2, Mars @ Windworks, Jupiter @ Eterna Building. |
| One Mega per side | The Spear Pillar double is **one** enemy side, so only Jupiter carries a stone there. |
| Gen 4 aces have no Mega form | Correct for all six of these bosses, so the Mega always rides a **non-ace teammate**. |
| Mega Stone costs the item slot | Every `isMega` member below holds its stone instead of a berry/Life Orb — the free difficulty offset from `mega-design.md` §4.7. |

## Balance warnings

**W1 — Barry R1 (Route 201) is unwinnable as placed â€” the one fight I could not make fair**

The rules require 6 Pokemon with the ace at the cap in force, and the cap in force at Route 201 is **18**. The player arrives at Route 201 with **one starter at level 5** and no Poke Balls. 6x L15-18 against 1x L5 is not hard, it is arithmetically impossible, and no moveset or item choice fixes it.

I emitted the roster as specified rather than silently deviating. Pick one of these before shipping:

1. **Recommended â€” demote R1 out of the boss set.** Keep it as the vanilla scripted 1-v-1 (Barry's starter at L5-8, party of 1) and treat `barry_r2_route_203` as his first real 6-mon fight. The 6-mon rule then applies to *boss* fights, which R1 is not.
2. **Relocate R1** to Route 202 / Jubilife, after the catching tutorial and the first Poke Ball purchase, and expect the player to have 3-4 Pokemon near the cap.
3. Accept it as an **unloseable scripted defeat** (a story beat, not a fight).

Everything else in this document is beatable. This one is not.

**W2 — Barry R1 and R2 cannot escalate â€” no cap raise sits between them**

Both Route 201 and Route 203 are pre-Roark, so both sit at cap **18** and both aces are Monferno 18. Under the old vanilla-scaled table they were L5 and L9 â€” a visible step. Now they are identical in level and can only be differentiated by items, moves and one evolved slot, which is what I did (R2 gets held items with real effects, Roselia for coverage, and Staravia at the cap too).
