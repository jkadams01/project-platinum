# Evolution audit — what cannot be done in this game, and what to do about it

Generated by `tools/_work/evo/audit.py` + `gen_doc.py` over `data/species.json`.
Re-run both after any change to the evolution data; every number below is derived, not typed.

**Premise.** Single-player only. No trading, no link cable, no online, no plans for any.
An evolution that cannot be performed is not a cosmetic gap — it permanently deletes a
Pokémon from the game.

Scizor, Kingdra, Milotic, Weavile, Lucario, Porygon-Z, Dusknoir, Rhyperior, Electivire,

species the Platinum dex is *about*.

Cross-checked against `data/rom/encounters.json` (183 zones, 195 distinct wild species):
only Raticate, Chansey, Steelix, Roselia and Chimecho survive a broken evolution route by
being catchable in the wild anyway.

## 3. Category by category

### 3.1 `trade` — plain trade (8 edges)

Kadabra→Alakazam, Machoke→Machamp, Graveler→Golem, Haunter→Gengar, Boldore→Gigalith,
Gurdurr→Conkeldurr, Phantump→Trevenant, Pumpkaboo→Gourgeist. **Owner rule 1** applies:
level up at a certain level. See §4 for why that level is 37.

### 3.2 `trade` + `heldItem` — trade holding an item (16 edges)

**Owner rule 2** applies: level up while holding said item, with *no* level threshold —
which is exactly what the owner’s own reference ROM does (game-design.md §5.5: Gen 4
methods `18`+`19`, "level-up holding that item, day and night"). This is the best of the
four rules under a hard level cap because it is **cap-neutral**: the item is the gate, so
no evolution level has to be invented and nothing is pushed past a cap.

### 3.3 `trade` + `tradeSpecies` — trade for a specific species (2 edges)

Karrablast↔Shelmet, both directions. Rule 1 applies to both halves.
(A `partySpecies` route — "evolves if the other one is in your party" — would be closer to
the canon flavour and the engine needs `partySpecies` anyway for Mantyke, but the owner
asked for a level, so a level is what §4 gives them.)

### 3.4 `timeOfDay` — 20 edges, and there is no clock

**Verified, not assumed.** `src/systems/encounters.gd:50` declares
`var time_of_day: String = ""` and *nothing in `src/` ever assigns it*; the only writer is
`tests/test_overworld.gd`. There is no game clock anywhere in the codebase — the only
`Time.*` calls are tick timers, the logger and the save timestamp. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`
lists day/night encounter substitution as intended, so a clock may arrive, but today
every one of these 20 edges is dead.

16 of the 20 are fixed by **deleting the `timeOfDay` key and changing nothing else** —
the rest of the condition already works. The other 4 are the dangerous ones, because
`timeOfDay` is the *only* thing separating two branches of one species:

| species | branch A | branch B | separated only by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eevee | Espeon `happiness 160` | Umbreon `happiness 160` | day vs night |
| Sneasel | Weavile `heldItem razor-claw` | Sneasler `heldItem razor-claw` | night vs day |

Drop `timeOfDay` naively and **one of each pair becomes unreachable** — the engine will
match whichever edge it happens to test first. Recommended replacements, both fully
single-player and needing no new system:

* **Espeon = `use-item sun-stone`, Umbreon = `use-item moon-stone`.** Both stones are
  unused on Eevee, the thematic fit is exact, and no clock is required.
* **Weavile keeps `heldItem razor-claw`** (the vanilla-canon route, and Sneasel is a
  Platinum native); **Sneasler becomes a plain `level-up` at L45 with no item.** A Sneasel
  holding a Razor Claw becomes Weavile at its next level-up; one that is not holding one
  reaches 45 and becomes Sneasler. Deterministic, and it needs the precedence rule below.

`timeOfDay: "full-moon"` (Ursaring→Ursaluna) is impossible on a second count: there is no
lunar phase either. Drop the condition and keep `use-item peat-block`.

### 3.5 `location` — 7 edges, and not one names a Sinnoh location

The brief asked whether Magnezone and Probopass are fine because Mt. Coronet’s magnetic
field exists. **Checked: they do not use Mt. Coronet.** The data says
`location: "blush-mountain"` — Alola’s Blush Mountain. All seven locations are Alolan or
Galarian: `blush-mountain` x2, `mount-lanakila` x2, `lush-jungle`, `vast-poni-canyon`,
`dusty-bowl`. There is also no `location` trigger in the engine at all.

Six of the seven are **harmlessly redundant** — every target already has a working stone
route (Magnezone/Probopass/Vikavolt via Thunder Stone, Leafeon via Leaf Stone, Glaceon and
Crabominable via Ice Stone), which is how Sword/Shield and Scarlet/Violet do it. So
**delete them; do not convert them.**

> **The single most dangerous mistake available here.** Blanket-converting every impossible
> edge to a bare `level-up` would give Eevee two `level-up` edges with no level and no
> condition (Leafeon and Glaceon). Every Eevee in the game would then evolve into one of
> them on its next level-up, destroying all eight other Eeveelutions. Rule 4 must not be
> applied mechanically — a redundant impossible edge is deleted, not converted.

The seventh, `dusty-bowl` on Yamask→Runerigus, rides on `take-damage` and is a real
problem — see §3.8.

### 3.7 Overqwil is unobtainable four ways, and one of them only shows up in the learnsets

Qwilfish has **three** edges to Overqwil. Two are Legends: Arceus move-style triggers and
obviously dead. The third, `level-up` + `knownMove: "barb-barrage"`, looks completely fine
— `knownMove` is an ordinary, implementable condition and Barb Barrage exists in
`moves.json`. Cross-checking `data/learnsets.json` is what kills it:

* Barb Barrage is learnable by exactly **two** species in the entire game.
* Qwilfish learns it as an **egg move only** — no level-up entry, no TM, no tutor.
* The only non-egg learner in the game is **Overqwil itself**, at L28.

So breeding for it is circular: you need an Overqwil to father the Qwilfish that knows the
move that makes an Overqwil. This is the kind of lockout that a method-string audit cannot
see, and it is why every `knownMove` edge was checked against the learnsets. The other 13
all pass — 12 by level-up, one (`dragon-cheer` for Hydrapple) by TM, which is a TM-
placement dependency worth noting but not a lockout.

### 3.8 Three same-species branch collisions

These are the only places where the owner’s four rules do not mechanically resolve, because
two edges of one species would end up matching at the same instant.

1. **Slowpoke.** `level-up L37 → Slowbro` already exists; rule 2 would add
   `level-up heldItem kings-rock → Slowking`. At L37 a King’s-Rock-holding Slowpoke matches
   both. game-design.md §5.5 already flags this as "the only genuinely ambiguous case" and
   records the reference ROM’s answer: make Slowking `use-item water-stone` instead.
   Either that, or adopt the precedence rule below. **Owner decision.**
2. **Sneasel.** Weavile and Sneasler, same item, separated only by day/night (§3.4).
3. **Yamask.** `level-up L34 → Cofagrigus` already exists. Runerigus **cannot** become a
   plain level-up at any level: lower than 34 and every Yamask becomes Runerigus; higher
   and no Yamask survives to reach it. It needs a non-level discriminator — give Runerigus
   its own `use-item` item (the pattern Charcadet and Applin already use), or delete the
   edge and accept one missing dex entry. **Owner decision.**

> **Recommended engine contract, which falls out of all three:** when more than one
> evolution edge of a species matches at the same moment, the **more specific** edge wins —
> held-item > other condition > bare level. With that rule, cases 1 and 2 resolve
> themselves and no data gymnastics are needed.

### 3.9 The three edges that look fine and are not

Pawmo→Pawmot, Bramblin→Brambleghast and Rellor→Rabsca are `method: "level-up"` with **no**
`level` and **no** condition. In canon all three are "walk 1000 steps with it out of its
ball"; the veekun dump has no column for that, so the trigger is simply *gone*. An engine
reading these either never fires them or fires them at level 2. A method-count audit files
them under the 395 healthy `level-up` edges. They need a level assigned (§4).

## 4. Recommended replacements, and why each level

### The cap curve is the constraint

A Pokémon at or above the active cap gains zero EXP, so **an evolution level is an
availability gate**. The ladder:

| segment | cap | | segment | cap |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| start / Roark | 18 | | Volkner | 90 |
| Gardenia | 27 | | Aaron | 96 |
| Fantina | 36 | | Bertha | 97 |
| Maylene | 45 | | Flint | 98 |
| Wake | 54 | | Lucian | 99 |
| Byron | 66 | | Cynthia | 100 |
| Candice | 78 | | post-game | 100 |

Two facts that decide the numbers:

1. **Level 37 is the cheapest level that is still "after gym 3".** It is one above the
   Fantina cap (36), so a trade evolution set to 37 becomes available the instant the Relic
   Badge raises the cap to 45 — and it then has 8 levels of headroom inside that segment.
   It is also the value the owner’s own reference ROM uses, verified by a NARC diff
   (game-design.md §5.5), so rule 1 and the existing research agree.
2. **A level equal to a cap is reachable.** The cap stops *further* gain; a Pokémon at 44
   under a cap of 45 still levels to 45 and then stops. So 45 is a legal target level.

It also lands where the balance work already wanted it. game-design.md §5.2 bans
Fighting/Ground/Steel before Roark and Dark/Ghost before Fantina; L37 keeps Machamp
(Fighting), Golem (Rock/Ground), Gengar and Trevenant (Ghost) out of exactly those windows
without a single extra rule. And the existing data tops out at L64 (Zweilous→Hydreigon),
with only 5 edges above 54 — so nothing proposed here is an outlier on the curve.

## 6. Two prerequisites this audit uncovered that are not evolution bugs

### 6.1 `data/items.json` contains no evolution items at all

It holds **93 entries: 92 Mega Stones and the Key Stone.** The evolution data references
**40 distinct items** (23 `item`, 17 `heldItem`) and **0 of them exist**. So today every
one of the 64 `use-item` evolutions is as dead as the trade evolutions — for a completely
different reason.

This is out of scope for an evolution fix and in scope for whoever owns `items.json`, but
it has to be said plainly: **converting the trade evolutions to hold-and-level moves them
from one blocker to another unless the 17 held items ship too.** Required, minimum:

* 10 ordinary stones: Fire, Water, Thunder, Leaf, Moon, Sun, Shiny, Dusk, Dawn, Ice.
* 17 hold-and-level items: King’s Rock, Metal Coat, Dragon Scale, Up-Grade, Dubious Disc,
  Protector, Electirizer, Magmarizer, Reaper Cloth, Deep Sea Tooth, Deep Sea Scale,
  Prism Scale, Sachet, Whipped Dream, Razor Claw, Razor Fang, Oval Stone.
  All `holdable: true`, `consumable: false`.
* 13 bespoke Gen 7–9 items, or the evolutions that need them stay unreachable: Black
  Augurite, Peat Block, Tart/Sweet/Syrupy Apple, Cracked Pot, Unremarkable Teacup, Metal
  Alloy, Auspicious Armor, Malicious Armor, Scroll of Darkness, Galarica Cuff, Galarica Wreath.

### 6.2 Three veekun dedupe artifacts quietly changed a vanilla route

`pokemon_evolution.csv` is per-version-group and the build keeps the highest version group
per `(target, trigger)` (species-data.md §5b). Where a regional form shares a species id
with its Kantonian original, the newer regional row therefore **wins**:

| species | data says | vanilla Platinum | effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vulpix → Ninetales | `use-item ice-stone` | Fire Stone | Ninetales is now an **Ice Stone** evolution. Only lockout risk of the three: there is no surviving Fire Stone route. |
| Meowth → Persian | `level-up happiness 160` | L28 | Persian is now a **friendship** evolution. Meowth also has L28 → Perrserker, so a low-friendship Meowth becomes Perrserker. Both reachable. |
| Slowpoke → Slowbro | L37 **and** `galarica-cuff` | L37 | Harmless — the L37 row survived alongside. |

Sandshrew (L22 + Ice Stone), Voltorb (L30 + Leaf Stone) and Darumaka (L35 + Ice Stone) all
kept their vanilla route as well, because level-up and use-item are different triggers and
the dedupe key includes the trigger. Vulpix is the one case where both rows were the same
trigger and the older one lost. **Owner decision:** add a Fire Stone route back, or accept
Ice Stone.

## 7. Scope interpretation, as instructed

The owner’s rule 3 reads "anything involving an item, just make it level up holding said
item". Applied literally that would convert all 64 stone evolutions into hold-and-level,
which would make the game *worse*: using a Fire Stone on a Growlithe already works
perfectly well in a single-player game. **Rule 3 is therefore read as applying only to
item-based evolutions that are impossible without it** — in practice the 16 trade-with-item
edges, which rule 2 already covers.

Genuinely item-impossible edges found: **zero.** Every bespoke item in the data (Black
Augurite, Peat Block, the Apples, Cracked Pot, Metal Alloy, the Armors, Teacup, Scroll of
Darkness, Galarica Cuff/Wreath) is an ordinary bag item that can simply be placed in
Sinnoh. The only item-shaped impossibility is the `full-moon` *condition* attached to the
Peat Block, not the Peat Block itself. So nothing was converted under that heading, and
§6.1 is the real item problem.
